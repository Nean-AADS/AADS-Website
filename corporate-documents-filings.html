Certificates of Good Standing, Filings and Entity Records

  • Certificates of good standing, including equivalent certificates of existence or status offered by the issuing office.
  • Plain and certified copies of formation documents and other specified filings.
  • Formations, foreign qualifications, amendments, annual reports and other business filings.
  • Registered agent appointments and changes.
  • Business publication notices for formations, foreign qualifications and assumed names (DBAs).
  • Document retrieval, apostilles, authentication and legalization for international use.

Prepare Your Request

  • Exact legal entity name, formation state and file number if known.
  • The receiving party’s exact document or filing requirement.
  • Plain or certified copies; short- or long-form certificate of good standing where offered.
  • Any issuance-date requirement, destination country and date needed.
  • Authorized filing instructions and contact details for any agent appointment.

Filings Across the Entity Lifecycle

  • Business formations, name reservations and name-change filings.
  • Foreign qualification for a specified entity and destination state.
  • Amendments, annual reports and reinstatement requests.
  • Merger, conversion, dissolution and withdrawal submissions where available.
  • Certificates of good standing and plain or certified copies of specified corporate filings.

Identify the entity, requested action and filing state. Required approvals, forms, supporting documents, government fees and anticipated timing are confirmed for the specific request. Choosing a legal structure or deciding where an entity must qualify belongs with the client’s advisers.

Business Publication Services

Business notices, newspaper placement and proof of publication.

Some jurisdictions require newspaper notices for business formations, registrations to do business in another state, or assumed names (DBAs). Requirements vary by entity type and location, including eligible newspapers, publication schedules, deadlines and any required follow-up filings.

AADS arranges newspaper publication, obtains publication affidavits and submits any required publication certificates. We confirm the applicable requirements, work, fees and expected timing before starting.

Answers for Your Next Request

What corporate compliance services does AADS support?

AADS provides certificates of good standing, corporate records, plain and certified copies, formations, foreign qualifications, amendments, annual reports and other business filings, plus registered agent appointments and changes. Requirements and availability are confirmed for each state and request.

Can AADS help obtain certificates of good standing?

Yes. Send the exact entity name, issuing state, file number if known, receiving party’s requirements and deadline. AADS obtains the certificate of good standing or equivalent certificate available from that office.

Is a certificate of good standing the same as a certified copy?

No. A certificate of good standing addresses entity status as defined by the issuing office. A certified copy reproduces a specified filed document with that office’s certification. Provide the receiving party’s exact requirement.

Can I request foreign qualification, annual reports or entity changes?

Yes. Identify the entity, formation jurisdiction, state where the filing is needed and the requested action. Provide the file number if known, authorized instructions, supporting documents and deadline. AADS confirms the filing requirements, fees and anticipated timing for that request.
