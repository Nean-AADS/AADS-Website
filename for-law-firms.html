For paralegals: move the request forward

  1. Choose the service. Request a search, court or corporate document, UCC filing or business filing below.
  2. Send the checklist. Include names, jurisdictions, copies and date needed; attach your instructions.
  3. Keep the reference. A successful submission returns an on-screen receipt and status link.
  4. Check progress. Review received, clarification, research, review and delivery updates when staff post them. Ask for an update using the same reference.
  5. Receive the evidence. Results, requested copies, source dates and outstanding items are identified for your file.

For attorneys: see the research behind the review

  • Coverage: the names, record types, period and offices included.

  • Evidence: requested search responses, filing histories, dockets and copies.

Answers for Your Request

Can related requests use one matter reference?

Yes. Send your matter reference and list each name, jurisdiction, search and document you need. You can request the related work through one point of contact.

Can I request a particular complaint, order or judgment?

Yes. Provide the court, case number and docket entry or document description if known. Identify certification and copy requirements; availability and any copy charges are confirmed for the request.

What will our team receive for review?

You receive the search results and copies you ordered, plus filing acknowledgments or other document evidence when included. We identify the sources searched and any items still pending. Before starting, we confirm the names, jurisdictions, record types and documents you need.
